# Pulp Riot Juice Co. — Standard Operating Procedure: Finished Goods Freshness & Depot Aging
**Document ID:** SOP-LOG-INV-003 | **Revision:** 2.4 | **Effective:** Summer Season 2027

## 1. Purpose & Freshness Windows
Pulp Riot juices feature a 90-day physical shelf-life. However, Modern Trade (MT) and Quick Commerce (QC) buyers reject shipments with less than 60 days remaining (Day 30 Commercial Rejection Cliff).

## 2. Guardrails & Automated Actions
- Maximum acceptable defect rate: <= 2.5% of network stock in at-risk or rejected status.
- Day 18 Warning (KPI D2): When any batch reaches Day 18 and regional run-rate cannot clear it within 7 days, the system must trigger an inter-depot transfer (₹1.50/bottle) or reroute to General Trade.
- Day 30 Critical Cliff (KPI D3): Mandatory write-off or liquidation discount.
