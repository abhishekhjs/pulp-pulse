# Pulp Riot Juice Co. — Standard Operating Procedure: Bottling Operations & Puree Sourcing
**Document ID:** SOP-OPS-SUP-002 | **Revision:** 4.1 | **Effective:** Summer Season 2027

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This SOP governs production scheduling, co-packer activation, and raw puree buffer management across Nashik and Pune bottling facilities. The target is maintaining plant utilization between 85% and 92%, with a maximum defect rate (unfulfilled or throttled production batches) below 3.0%.

## 2. Process Specifications & Ceilings
- In-house bottling capacity ceiling: 95.0% maximum sustained utilization.
- Co-Packer notice requirement: Minimum 14 days advance booking for +10% production flex (+3,800 bottles/day).
- Puree storage stability: -18°C frozen pulp holds for 180 days.
- Reorder Point: Minimum 25 days cover required before peak season (April 15).

## 3. Defect Classification
1. **Pulp_Stockout_Throttle**: Line running at <60% capacity due to ingredient stockout.
2. **Capacity_Exhaustion_Bottleneck**: Orders dropped due to >95% plant utilization.
3. **Unplanned_SKU_Switchover**: Idle time exceeding 120 minutes during flavor changeover.

## 4. Root Causes & Escalation Protocols
Whenever passionfruit cover drops below 14 days or plant utilization crosses 93%, the Supply Lead must authorize co-packer booking and throttle low-velocity Mango production lines immediately.
