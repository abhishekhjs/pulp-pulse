# Pulp Riot Juice Co. — Standard Operating Procedure: Cold Chain Logistics & Sourcing Transit
**Document ID:** SOP-LOG-TRN-004 | **Revision:** 3.1 | **Effective:** Summer Season 2027

## 1. Cold Chain Temperature Mandates
- Reefer fleet setpoint: 3.0°C (operational tolerance: 2.0°C to 4.0°C).
- Any temperature excursion exceeding 4.5°C triggers immediate alert and mandatory secondary QC audit.

## 2. Inbound Landslide Risk Mitigation
- Highway transit from Himalayan passionfruit cooperative takes 3 days with no alternative road route.
- Prior to April 15, frozen pulp inventory in Nashik plant cold storage must be built to 35 days cover.
- Emergency air freight charters are strictly prohibited unless authorized by the COO and verified by KPI A4.
