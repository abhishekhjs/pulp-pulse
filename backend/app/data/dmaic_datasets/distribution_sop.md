# Pulp Riot Juice Co. — Standard Operating Procedure: Regional Depot Balancing & Allocation
**Document ID:** SOP-LOG-DST-005 | **Revision:** 2.8 | **Effective:** Summer Season 2027

## 1. Depot Allocation Architecture
Pulp Riot operates 9 regional cold depots across 4 zones: North (N1 Delhi, N2 Chandigarh), West (W1 Mumbai, W2 Pune, W3 Ahmedabad), South (S1 Bengaluru, S2 Chennai, S3 Hyderabad), East (E1 Kolkata).

## 2. Dynamic Balancing Thresholds
- Target depot cover: 10 to 14 days of dynamic demand cover.
- Stock Imbalance Index (KPI E1) trigger: If regional variance exceeds 8.0%, immediate transfer recommendation is generated.
- Inter-depot transit cost standard: ₹1.50 per bottle with 2-4 days transit window.
